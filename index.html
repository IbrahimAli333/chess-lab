Engine: Stockfish 19 lite, built for the web by Stockfish.js (GPLv3, source at github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js and github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish). It runs on several threads when your browser allows it, with a single-threaded build and Stockfish 10 as fallbacks. Everything runs in your browser. Engine in use: starts with your first review. Deep check loads the full Stockfish 19 network from unpkg.com (npm package stockfish 19.0.1, GPLv3) only when you press it. Move logic: chess.js (BSD 2-Clause). Pieces by Colin M.L. Burnett (CC BY-SA 3.0). Opening names: lichess-org/chess-openings (CC0). Ratings follow chess.com's style but are not identical to it.
